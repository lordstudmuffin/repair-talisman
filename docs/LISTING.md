# Repair Talisman (CurseForge / Modrinth listing text)

**One craftable talisman. Everything you carry repairs itself.**

Repair Talisman adds a single item: a gold-and-amethyst charm with a diamond at its heart. Keep it anywhere in your
inventory and every few seconds it restores a point of durability on every damaged item you have on you: the tools in
your hotbar, the armor you are wearing, the spare gear in your backpack rows and whatever is in your offhand.

- **Standalone.** Needs Fabric Loader only. No Fabric API, no Trinkets, no library mods.
- **Lightweight.** One item, one recipe, two tiny mixins, one properties file.
- **Fair.** Craft it from 4 amethyst shards, 4 gold ingots and 1 diamond. Rate and slots are configurable.
- **Plays nice.** Works with Mending, Unbreaking, elytra, shields and modded tools; carrying two talismans does not
  stack.

Recipe: amethyst shard in the corners, gold ingot on the edges, diamond in the middle.

Built for Minecraft 26.1.x on Fabric Loader 0.18+. The old repair-charm mods stopped at 1.21.1; this one is written
fresh for 26.1.
